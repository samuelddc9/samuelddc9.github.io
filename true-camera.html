About True Camera

Sign your photos and videos to prove they are real and not AI generated. In an era where AI-generated content is becoming increasingly sophisticated, True Camera provides a way to authenticate your media and establish trust.

Key Features

  • Digital signature for photos and videos
  • AI detection and verification
  • Easy-to-use mobile interface
  • Secure authentication system
  • Proof of authenticity

How It Works

True Camera uses advanced cryptographic signatures to verify that your photos and videos are authentic and not generated by AI. When you capture media with True Camera, it's automatically signed with a unique digital signature that proves its authenticity. This helps establish trust in a world where AI-generated content is becoming increasingly common.
