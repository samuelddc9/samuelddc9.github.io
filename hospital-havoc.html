About Hospital Havoc

Hospital Havoc is a fast-paced cooperative game where players team up to manage a chaotic hospital. Work together with your friends to treat patients, manage resources, and keep the hospital running smoothly as chaos ensues!

Key Features

  • Cooperative multiplayer gameplay
  • Fast-paced action and strategy
  • Multiple game modes and challenges
  • Progressive difficulty system
  • Engaging hospital management mechanics

Gameplay

In Hospital Havoc, players must work together to manage a busy hospital. Each player takes on different roles - from doctors to nurses to administrators - and must coordinate their actions to keep patients healthy and the hospital functioning. As the game progresses, new challenges arise that require teamwork and quick thinking to overcome.
