January 5, 2024 Behind the Scenes

The Art of Monster Medbay

Take a look behind the scenes at how we created the quirky monsters and vibrant hospital setting for Monster Medbay. From initial sketches to final animations, discover the creative process behind our latest game.

The visual design of Monster Medbay was a collaborative effort between our concept artists, 3D modelers, and animators. We wanted to create a world that felt both whimsical and slightly chaotic, perfectly matching the game's fast-paced cooperative gameplay.

Our concept artists started with rough sketches of various monster designs. We explored different styles - from cute and cuddly to quirky and strange. The goal was to create characters that players would find endearing, even when they're causing chaos in the hospital.

The hospital setting itself went through several iterations. We wanted it to feel like a real medical facility, but with a playful twist. Bright colors, rounded corners, and friendly signage help create an inviting atmosphere that contrasts with the frantic gameplay.

Animation was crucial for bringing these characters to life. Each monster has unique movement patterns and expressions that reflect their personality. The medical staff animations needed to be clear and readable so players could quickly understand what actions were being performed.

We're incredibly proud of how Monster Medbay looks and feels, and we can't wait for players to experience this vibrant world we've created!

December 28, 2023 Updates

Sync Slayer: Rhythm Meets Combat

We're excited to share more details about Sync Slayer, our upcoming rhythm-action game. Learn about how we're combining music synchronization with strategic combat mechanics to create a unique gaming experience.

Sync Slayer represents a unique fusion of rhythm gameplay and action combat. The core concept is simple: your attacks are more powerful when synchronized with the beat of the music, but timing is everything.

We've been working closely with our audio team to create a dynamic soundtrack that responds to player actions. The music isn't just background - it's an integral part of the gameplay. Each enemy has its own rhythm pattern, and players need to learn these patterns to maximize their effectiveness in combat.

The combat system combines real-time action with rhythm-based mechanics. Players can perform basic attacks at any time, but special moves and combos require precise timing with the music. This creates a skill-based gameplay loop where players are rewarded for both their combat prowess and their sense of rhythm.

We're also implementing a variety of weapons and abilities, each with their own rhythm patterns. Some weapons might have a fast, staccato rhythm, while others follow a slower, more deliberate beat. This variety ensures that players can find a playstyle that matches their preferences.

Sync Slayer is shaping up to be one of our most innovative projects yet, and we're excited to share more details as development continues!

Horizon Breach: Board Game Launch

We're thrilled to announce the launch of Horizon Breach, our cooperative board game featuring mechas and kaiju. Join us as we defend humanity in this strategic tabletop adventure!

After months of development and playtesting, we're excited to officially launch Horizon Breach! This cooperative board game brings together everything we love about strategic gameplay and epic battles.

In Horizon Breach, players take on the roles of elite mecha pilots working together to defend humanity from colossal kaiju threats. Each player controls a unique mecha with special abilities, and success requires careful coordination and strategic planning.

The game features a modular board system that creates different battlefields for each playthrough. The kaiju AI system ensures that no two games are ever the same, keeping the experience fresh and challenging.

We've included multiple difficulty levels, from beginner-friendly scenarios to expert challenges that will test even the most experienced players. The game supports 2-4 players and typically takes 60-90 minutes to play.

Horizon Breach is now available for purchase through our website and select retailers. We're incredibly proud of this project and can't wait to see players experience the thrill of piloting mechas against massive kaiju!

December 15, 2023 Development

Building Cooperative Gameplay Mechanics

Cooperation is at the heart of everything we do at Team Tap Games. In this post, we dive deep into the design philosophy behind our cooperative gameplay mechanics and how we create experiences that bring players together.

At Team Tap Games, we believe that the best gaming experiences happen when players work together. Our design philosophy centers around creating mechanics that encourage communication, coordination, and genuine teamwork.

One of the key principles we follow is ensuring that no single player can succeed alone. Each player needs to contribute their unique abilities and perspectives to overcome challenges. This creates a sense of shared accomplishment and makes victories feel truly earned.

We also design our games to reward positive communication. Players who work together and share information are more likely to succeed, creating a natural incentive for collaboration. This doesn't mean we eliminate individual skill - rather, we create systems where individual excellence enhances the team's overall performance.

Another important aspect is managing difficulty. Cooperative games can be tricky to balance - too easy and players don't feel challenged, too hard and frustration sets in. We use extensive playtesting to find that sweet spot where players feel challenged but not overwhelmed.

Finally, we always consider the social aspect of our games. Whether it's couch co-op or online multiplayer, we want our games to be experiences that players remember and want to share with others. That's the true power of cooperative gameplay - it creates lasting memories and strengthens friendships.
