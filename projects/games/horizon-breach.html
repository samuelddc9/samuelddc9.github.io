About Horizon Breach

In a world under siege by colossal kaiju, teams of elite pilots must join forces, piloting powerful mechas to protect humanity. Cooperation and strategy are key as players work together to outsmart and defeat monstrous threats, defending the last remnants of civilization.

Key Features

  • Cooperative team-based gameplay
  • Strategic mecha combat system
  • Epic kaiju battles
  • Modular board system
  • Multiple difficulty levels

Gameplay

Horizon Breach is a cooperative board game where 2-4 players work together as elite mecha pilots defending humanity from massive kaiju threats. Each player controls a unique mecha with special abilities, and success requires careful coordination and strategic planning. The modular board creates different battlefields for each playthrough, and the kaiju AI ensures no two games are ever the same.
