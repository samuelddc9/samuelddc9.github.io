About Kick Soccer Card Game

Simple and fun soccer themed card game to play with your friends. Perfect for quick games and bringing the excitement of soccer to your tabletop!

Key Features

  • Easy to learn, quick to play
  • Soccer-themed gameplay
  • Perfect for friends and family
  • Portable card game format
  • Engaging strategic elements

Gameplay

Kick Soccer Card Game brings the excitement of soccer to your tabletop. With simple rules and fast-paced gameplay, it's perfect for game nights with friends. The card-based mechanics make it easy to learn while still offering strategic depth for those who want to master the game.
