About Monster Medbay

Monster Medbay is a fast-paced cooperative game where players team up to manage a chaotic hospital treating quirky monsters. With a Rogue-lite twist, each playthrough offers new challenges and opportunities.

Key Features

  • Cooperative multiplayer gameplay
  • Rogue-lite progression system
  • Quirky monster patients to treat
  • Procedurally generated challenges
  • Unlockable upgrades and abilities

Gameplay

Work together with your team to treat a variety of unique monsters in a chaotic hospital setting. Each run is different thanks to the rogue-lite mechanics, ensuring fresh challenges every time you play. Coordinate your actions, manage resources, and keep the medbay running smoothly!
